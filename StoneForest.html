Stone Forest

The peculiar karst landscapes of Yunnan's Stone Forest are so cool, located just an hour from the bustling city of Kunming, making it one of the most accessible natural wonders in China and certainly one of the highlights of the city.

Within the Forest

The formations of Stone Forest are so diverse, with plenty of great hiking and viewpoints to offer. My favorite has to be the highest viewpoint overlooking the entire Stone Forest, making the whole landscape look otherworldly. Other cool formations can be found across the forest, including massive clusters rocks scattered around the magical emerald green landscape.
