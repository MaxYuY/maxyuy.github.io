Maui Reefs

The reefs and beaches of Maui are paradise, with tons to do from relaxing on the beach itself to exploring the underwater world and its incredible marine life.

Snorkeling has to be my favorite thing I did on the island, which my favorite spots being Honokeana Bay (left), Kapalua Bay (center), Coral Gardens (right) which is only accessible by tour, Honolua Bay, and Kahekeli Beach Park, all found on the western side of the island.

Besides coral reefs, Maui is one of the best places in the entire world to swim with beautiful and adorable sea turtles in their natural habitat, especially in Honokeana Bay where turtles are literally everywhere in the water.

Sea Turtles are also commonly seen on the beaches themselves, especially at the Ho'okipa Beach Park, where hundreds of them can be found on the beach at the exact same time, which was incredible to witness.
