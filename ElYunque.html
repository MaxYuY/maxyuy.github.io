El Yunque National Forest

El Yunque National Forest is one of the most popular places to visit in Puerto Rico, and for good reason. The park is home to pristine rainforests and plenty of stunning views of the main island of Puerto Rico accessible by trail, making the park a fantastic adventure destination.

On my visit to El Yunque, I did the Torre Britton Trail after passing by the Catarata Coca Waterfall. The trail brings you up the brilliant nearby peaks to this stunning overlook of the island, with views of Eastern Puerto rico and Luquillo town being the highlights.
