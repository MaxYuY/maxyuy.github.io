China

Just like the U.S, China is also incredibly diverse when it comes to landscapes. With deserts and mountains to the west, grassland and basins in middle, and vast open plains in the east, there's so much to see and do there. The alpine valleys of Jiuzhaigou and Huanglong National Parks in Sichuan, the Jiuxiang Caves and Jade Dragon Snow Mountain in Yunnan, and the Summer Palace and Great Wall of China in Beijing are just a few of the wide variety of places to visit in this amazing country.
