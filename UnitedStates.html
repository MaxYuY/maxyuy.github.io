United States

The U.S. is one of the most beautiful countries in the world in terms of landscapes. From the deserts and rocky mountains in the west to the temperate forests and wetlands in the east, it has every type of landscape available and tons of things to see and do. The world famous canyons like Arizona's Grand Canyon, the mountain vistas of Montana's Glacier National Park, and alpine lakes like Lake Tahoe are some of many cool places in the country.

Within the stunning natural landscapes of the U.S resides some of the most iconic and unique wildlife species in the world. From large mammals such as bears, moose, mountain goats, and bison, to smaller creatures like ground squirrels, marmots, and rabbits, to aquatic animals like whales, seals, and sea otters, the United States is a widlife haven, and visiting places like Yellowstone or Rocky Mountain National Parks and Monterey Bay will guarantee many memorable wild animal encounters.
