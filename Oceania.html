Hawaii

The islands of Hawaii are literally paradise, full of stunning beach getaways and otherworldly landscapes. The volcanoes of Haleakala National Park in Maui and the colorful Waimea Canyon are just a few examples. Plus the Reefs of Maui and Reefs of Kauai, both the landscapes and seascapes here are so cool, making it some of my favorite islands to visit.

The wildlife of Hawaii is just as diverse as its scenery. On land, seeing the endangered Nenes or Hawaiian Geese in Haleakala National Park and Monk Seals on the beaches near Kauai's Reefs can be an unforgettable wildlife experience. Just as impressive are the huge number of Sea Turtles that can be found off the coast of Maui as well as huge pods of dolphins on a Napali Coast tour in Kauai. Thus, a visit to Hawaii would guarantee seeing some incredible and rare wildlife encounters on your visit.
