Hawaii

The islands of Hawaii are literally paradise, full of stunning beach getaways and otherworldly landscapes. The volcanoes of Haleakala National Park in Maui and the colorful Waimea Canyon are just a few examples. With the Reefs of Maui and Reefs of Kauai, both the landscapes and seascapes here are so cool, making it some of my favorite islands to visit.

Waimea Canyon

Haleakala NP

The wildlife of Hawaii is just as diverse as its scenery. On land, seeing the endangered Hawaiian Geese in Haleakala National Park and Monk Seals on the beaches near Kauai's Coast were unforgettable experiences. Just as impressive are the huge number of Sea Turtles that can be found in Maui's Reefs as well as huge pods of dolphins off the Napali Coast in Kauai.
