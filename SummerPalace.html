Summer Palace

The Summer Palace is one of Beijing's most iconic attractions. Located in the northwestern part of the city, the palace was the historical summer home of the Chinese emperor. Now though, the palace is still beautiful in all times of the year, being one of the best places to visit in Beijing.

Both on my summer visit in 2023 (Top) and my winter visit in 2024 (Bottom), walking around the park and exploring the stunning lakeside temple scenery and relics of ancient China was so cool, as was taking a boat onto the lake during the summer for some up close views of the massive temple and the 17-hole bridge (Top right). In winter, the freezed-up lake turns the park into a winter wonderland, with a different but equally magical feeling around the beautiful park.

The birdlife in Kunming Lake, is abundant, with my highlight has to be viewing these adorable ducklings on our boat ride while admiring the gorgeous palace scenery.
