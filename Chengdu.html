Chengdu

The city of Chengdu is one of my favorite cities in the world, as the symbol of the city is one of my favorite animals, the Panda, being called the Panda capital of the world.

There are 4 Panda bases around Chengdu, with the biggest one is located in the center of the city. This base in downtown Chengdu is home to over 100 adorable pandas, which I witnessed playing around and eating, one of the cutest things I've ever seen. The base also houses other animals like Red Pandas as well as majestic Black swans in the park's biggest lake.
