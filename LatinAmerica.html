Latin America

Latin America is home to some of the most extensive rainforests in the world as well as gorgeous tropical beach paradises. With the jungles of Costa Rica's La Fortuna, Puerto Rico's El Yunque, and the Amazon Rainforest in Brazil, as well as the vast beaches of Central America and the Carribean islands, including Isla Culebra in Puerto Rico and Costa Rica's Manuel Antonio National Park, Latin America is full of cool places to explore.

San Juan

Latin America is one of the most biodiverse regions in the entire world, home to plenty of amazing and unique wildlife. The Tapirs, Coati, and Anteaters of Corcovado National Park, the sloths and monkeys of Manuel Antonio National Park, and Sea Turtles of Isla Culebra are just some of the wildlife highlights in this beautiful region.
