Vancouver

Vancouver is one of the biggest cities in Canada and my favorite due to its gorgeous skyline and nature. There is plenty of things to do here and in the city's surroundings, from forest parks to incredible seaside views.

Stanley Park is the crown jewel of Vancouver, giving the best views of Vancouver's skyline. The totem poles are also a must-see here, representing the indigenous people and cultures that call the region home.

Outside the concrete jungle of Vancouver's wonderful skyline, the wildlife of Stanley Park is also abundant, with beautiful Wood Ducks, Green Frogs, and Beavers calling the urban forest home.
