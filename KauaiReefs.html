Kauai Reefs

While not as much as Maui, The reefs and beaches of Kauai are still very biodiverse, with tons of cool things to see and marine wildlife to find.

Southern Coast

Near Poipu Beach

Places such as Koloa Landing, Poipu Beach, and Lawai Beach are some of my favorite snorkeling spots on Kauai, featuring a really cool array of marine life and coral reefs that I was really impressed with.

In Kauai, there are tons of marine life on the beaches themselves. This included the famous daily turtle arrival on Poipu beach in the evening, which make their way back to sea at dawn. Besides turtles, Poipu beach is the common spot to see rare Hawaiian Monk Seals, which were often resting on the beach and on the nearby island.
