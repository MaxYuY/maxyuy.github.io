Monterey Bay

Monterey Bay in the central coast of California is a haven for aquatic wildlife, with plenty of diverse creatures calling the bay and its surroundings home.

Among the all the wildlife in Monterey, Sea Otters (Top Left, top center) were by far my favorite as they were just adorable. At both Monterey and Elkhorn Slough, I got to see tons of Sea Otters, some with their pups, hanging out right inside the bay. Some other notable aquatic mammals include Harbor Seals relaxing in Elkhorn Slough (Bottom Left, Bottom center) as well as tons of California sea lions (Top right, bottom right), which were literally everywhere in the town of Monterey Bay.

On a whale watching tour in Monterey, I got to spot a pod of Risso's dolphins (left) as well as a Humpback whale and her calf (center, right), some of the coolest aquatic animals on Earth.
