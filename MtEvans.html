Mt. Evans Scenic Byway

Reaching up to over 4300 meters above sea level, the Mt. Evans Scenic Byway in Colorado to its namesake mountain (now Mt. Blue Sky) is the highest paved road in the United States, containing fantastic scenery and plenty of wildlife opportunities.

The hiking at the top of the mountain is absolutely stunning, bringing great views of Summit Lake (Left, center) below as well as the other Front Range mountains (right) in the background.

While the mountain and alpine views are certainly spectacular, the wildlife along the byway is the highlight of the drive. My favorite wildlife species has to be the Mountain Goats (entire top row), as I saw multiple herds of goats and their adorable lambs in various points along the drive. Bighorn sheep (Bottom left images) are also abundant, especially around the Summit Lake area where they often roam on or near the road. Adorable Marmots (Bottom right images), squirrels, and pikas are also everywhere too, especially on the rocky trail up to the summit.
