Lake Tahoe

Located on the California-Nevada border, Lake Tahoe is one of the most famous lakes in the Western United States, with plenty of opportunities for adventure within the spectacular views of the Sierra Nevada mountains.

Emerald Bay

Sand Harbor State Park

During winter, skiing at Lake Tahoe is basically a must, with my favorite resort being Heavenly in both Nevada and California, which is by far the most scenic resort I've ever went to. The resort is unique, with views of the forests around Lake Tahoe on one side and the barren Nevada desert on the other, and the sheer contrast between the landscapes was awesome to see.

Emerald Bay state park on the shores of Lake Tahoe on the California side is awesome, with beautiful viewpoints of the bay as well as great hiking such as the Eagle Falls Trail. Seeing the giant lake with snowy mountains in the background was genuinely surreal.

The Nevada side of Lake Tahoe is equally stunning, with the highlights including Sand Harbor State Park and Memorial Point. Just like Emerald Bay, the crystal blue waters of the lake in front of the snowy Sierra Nevada mountains make them great places to admire this alpine wonderland.
